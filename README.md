EMPLOYEE ATTRITION ANALYSIS
Short Project Report
1. Introduction
Employee attrition is an important HR problem that can lead to loss of skilled employees, reduced
productivity, and increased recruitment costs. This project uses HR analytics and data visualization to
identify patterns and factors associated with employee attrition.
2. Objectives
• Analyze employee attrition patterns.
• Identify important factors contributing to attrition.
• Study departmental and demographic trends.
• Examine tenure, job role, salary, satisfaction, and work-life balance.
3. Data and Methodology
The analysis considers attributes such as age, gender, department, job role, monthly income, job
satisfaction, work-life balance, years at company, overtime, performance rating, and attrition status. The
preprocessing flow includes data collection, cleaning, missing-value handling, transformation, and
analysis.
Python tools such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn are used for data analysis and
visualization.
4. Visualization and Findings
Exploratory data analysis focuses on attrition rate, department and job-role patterns, tenure, age, salary,
satisfaction, and work-life balance. The project presentation highlights higher attrition risk during the first
two years of employment. Low salary, poor work-life balance, low satisfaction, and frequent overtime are
also identified as important factors in the analysis.
5. Conclusion
Employee attrition analysis helps organizations understand workforce risk and make evidence-based HR
decisions. Visualization makes these patterns easier to understand and supports targeted retention
strategies, compensation review, employee engagement programs, and workforce planning.
6. Future Scope
Future work can include AI-based attrition prediction, real-time employee engagement monitoring,
predictive risk modelling, HR dashboards, and machine-learning models for prioritizing retention actions
